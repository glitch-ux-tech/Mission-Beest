Earth is no longer a safe home for humanity. A rapidly developing planetary crisis has pushed civilization toward the edge of survival.

THE JOURNEY

Nova's body showed an unprecedented ability to adapt to alien planetary conditions. He was placed into long-term hibernation and launched into deep space. If he awakens successfully on another planet, humanity will achieve its first journey beyond Earth and Our last hope for survival..

The mission is simple: awaken Nova on another world, survive its conditions, and uncover what humanity needs to secure our future.
